Offline visitor QR booking

Place this QR code at hospital reception or the waiting area. Visitors scan it with their phone camera and open the HospiCare appointment page.

Set your public website URL

Before printing, enter the deployed public address of your HospiCare website. A localhost or file path will not work on visitors' phones.

Preview booking page
How it works

1. Visitor scans the QR code.
2. HospiCare opens the online booking page.
3. Visitor selects a doctor and slot.
4. Booking is saved and a token is generated.
5. Visitor follows the queue and opts in to turn alerts.
